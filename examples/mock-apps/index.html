MakerMods mock apps

Small applications built on the arm SDKs. Add ?mock=metal, ?mock=maker or ?mock=so101 to run one on simulated hardware (&adapters=2 plugs in two adapters, &link=slcan puts the arm behind an SLCAN adapter on a serial port) instead of a real adapter.

  • Arm console: connect, hold, move, sine, rest, e-stop (Metal or Maker).
  • Arm console on SLCAN: the same console on an SLCAN adapter over Web Serial.
  • Arm in a worker: the same arm and its control loop in a Web Worker.
  • Teleoperation: a Star Arm 102 leader (simulated, on sliders) drives a follower.
  • Arm-to-arm following: a floated leader arm and a follower on two adapters.
  • Two-arm robot: a rig of two arms driven as a group, bimanual sine and teleop.
  • Cameras: list, open, read frames and encode JPEGs with the SDK's Camera.
  • Loop timing bench: control-loop rate on simulated motors, in the page and in a worker.
  • SO-101: pick the serial port, calibrate, enable, move, wiggle, rest.
  • Mock playground: camera frames to a Dream policy through a proxy, chunks to the arm.
